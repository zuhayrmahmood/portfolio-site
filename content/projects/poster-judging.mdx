My sister hosts conferences pretty often, however, there is one problem: the judging process. Currently, she hands out sheets of paper to the judges
for them to fill out and return. This gets tedious really fast, sometimes there are judges that add up their totals wrong, sometimes they don't return their sheet *(and she has to chase them down)*. 
All in all, it's a mess. 

A few days ago, she asked me if I could her a platform to make the judging process easier, and I couldn't say no to the opportunity of learning something new.

## What it does

This platform allows judges to log in (via an auth code generated and sent to them via email by the organizer, *no one wants to make a pesky account for a single event*). 
After logging in, judges can submit their scores for each poster they were assigned (the organizer can assign posters), they submit it and ta-dah! The posters are all rated, and
the organizer isn't chasing down judges for their scores, correcting their math or doing a [mad minute](https://www.superteacherworksheets.com/math-drills-minute.html).
